Volume 14 — Notifications, Feedback, System States & User Communication

Purpose: Define every notification, toast, banner, alert, feedback state, and system communication pattern across MUSHIN. This ensures users always understand what is happening, what happened, why it happened, and what to do next.

14.1 Design Philosophy

Notifications should:

Never interrupt primary workflows unnecessarily.
Be informative before being decorative.
Prioritize clarity over cleverness.
Always provide a recovery path when possible.
Scale from micro feedback (button clicked) to critical system incidents.
Maintain trust by explaining failures honestly.

Every notification must answer:

What happened?
Why did it happen?
What should the user do next?

The frontend follows a modular, domain-driven architecture. Every feature owns its components, hooks, services, types, and utilities. Cross-feature dependencies are minimized to reduce coupling and simplify long-term maintenance.

 ├── utils/
 └── assets/

No feature imports another feature directly. Shared functionality belongs only in the shared layer.
